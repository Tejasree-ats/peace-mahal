About Peace Mahal

Peace Mahal is a functional and event venue located in Keelakarai, Tamil Nadu, providing a welcoming setting for weddings, receptions, family functions and special occasions. With its convenient location and event-focused environment, Peace Mahal is designed to bring families and guests together for meaningful celebrations.

Situated at 12/620, Vallal Seethakathi Salai, behind Best Mummy Bakery in Keelakarai (Kilakarai), the venue offers a peaceful, dedicated space featuring a spacious functional hall, an ornate ceremonial stage, high-capacity ventilation, and accessible parking for guests from across Ramanathapuram District.
